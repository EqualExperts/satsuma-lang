# viz: a field's metadata badges overflow the schema card instead of wrapping

Reported from a screenshot: an expanded overview card whose `email` field carries `pii`, `format email` and `mask email_to_domain` ran its badge strip past the card's right edge.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A field row's badges wrap onto further lines inside the card; no badge paints past the card edge.
- The overview layout reserves the wrapped height, so an expanded card never overlaps a neighbour.
- Playwright coverage in a real browser (harness.test.ts, "Compact card expansion in overview").

## Notes

**2026-10-06T13:19:01Z**

Cause: `.field-row` had a fixed height and `.badges` could neither shrink nor wrap. The overview layout's width estimate also ignored metadata pills, and its height estimate assumed 28px rows (34px rendered) and no field notes.
Fix: Rows take a min-height and the badge strip wraps. A new `field-badges.ts` gives the card and the layout one badge list. The expanded-card estimate now counts every badge, wrapped badge lines and field note rows. (commit immediately after 8fd59973)
